# 01: Cover for "Why Personal Ledger Uses VIPER"

Status: resolved
Type: task

Post: `content/blogs/2026-11-17-personal-ledger-viper/`. The brief asked for a variant of a viper, with three concepts to choose from.

## Answer

Three concepts were drafted: a segmented snake labelled V-I-P-E-R, a diamond-back pattern of file tiles, and a viper head beside a V-I-P-E-R legend. Ian picked the third, then asked for:

- a real viper photo instead of the geometric head: Gaboon viper (*Bitis gabonica*) by TimVickers, Wikimedia Commons, public domain (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bitis_gabonica_head.jpg); greyscale, embedded as base64
- the photo flipped so the viper faces the legend
- the V-I-P-E-R letters in the site's green `#1a7f37`
- Archivo rendered for real (the font was downloaded and set up just for the render) instead of the Liberation Sans fallback

Installed as `personal-ledger-viper-cover.svg` and a 2400 px `.png`, with a caption credit and link. Published on 2026-11-27 (`main` at `e9c0c79`).

Follow-ups done in the same session:

- `docs/agents/cover-image.md`: licensed images are now an option (PD, CC BY or CC BY-SA, credited in the caption), plus the green accent and how to render with Archivo.
- Theme: cover captions render Markdown, so credits can link to their source (`00d1759`, on `drafts`, not yet published).

Working files are archived in the session scratchpad as `viper-cover-work-2026-11-27.tar.gz`. The final SVG and PNG are in the post bundle.
